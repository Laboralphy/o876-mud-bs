# Skills

Skills represent learned competencies that sharpen the raw potential of the four abilities. A high Body score makes you strong; Athletics makes you a climber. A high Mind score makes you sharp; Arcana makes you a scholar of the arcane. Skills are not locked behind levels — they are acquired, practiced, and refined through play.

Each skill is anchored to one of the four core abilities. When a skill check is required, the governing ability modifier applies directly as a bonus or penalty.

---

## Based on BODY

### Athletics
Raw physical power applied to movement and effort — climbing, swimming, jumping, forcing open doors, carrying heavy loads. No technique, no finesse. Just the body doing what bodies do when pushed to their limit. Some locations are simply unreachable without adequate Athletic ability.

#### Actions that use Athletics
- **Climb** — Reaching rooms accessible only by vertical ascent or descent.
- **Swim** — Moving efficiently between underwater rooms.
- **Jump** — Crossing gaps to reach adjacent rooms with no direct path.
- **Bash** — Forcing open locked or stuck doors; reduces damage taken when breaking through barriers.

#### Passive uses of Athletics
- Maintaining grip and footing under physical duress.
- Augment carry weight
- Augment underwater autonomy

---

### Discipline
The capacity to endure. Pain, hunger, stun, exhaustion — and to keep functioning anyway. Not the absence of suffering, but the refusal to let it decide. Soldiers have it. Survivors have it. Most people discover too late that they don't.

#### Actions that use Discipline
- **Second Wind** — Recovering a portion of lost HP by drawing on willpower alone, even in a bad situation.

#### Passive uses of Discipline
- Reducing or delaying penalties from hunger, thirst, and exhaustion.
- Maintaining concentration on sustained actions under pressure (such as doing complex actions like casting spells, while poisoned, stunned, exhausted,).

---

### Survival
Staying alive in hostile environments through practical knowledge and physical resourcefulness. Finding water, building shelter, treating wounds with what is at hand. The world is trying to kill you. This skill is your argument against it.

#### Actions that use Survival
- **Forage** — Locating edible plants, clean water, or other natural resources in the field.
- **Harvest** — Extracting useful anatomy resources (organs, hides, venom) from dead creatures.
- **First Aid** — Using a medical kit more efficiently; higher skill yields better healing and fewer wasted supplies.

#### Passive uses of Survival
- Resting in the wild without alerting nearby hostile creatures.

---

### Martial Expertise
The mastery of advanced physical combat techniques — not just hitting harder, but hitting smarter. Where raw Body determines brute force, Martial Expertise determines whether that force is applied with precision, timing, and control. Veterans of prolonged, real combat have this. Tavern brawlers rarely do.

#### Actions that use Martial Expertise
- **Power Strike** — Sacrifice 5 points of attack bonus to add 5 points of damage. Trades accuracy for impact against low-AC targets.
- **Stunning Strike** — Use a shield bash, knee strike, weapon cross, or pommel blow to stun an opponent. Effective for crowd control or creating openings.
- **Disarm** — Force an opponent to drop their weapon with a precise wrist or grip strike.

#### Passive uses of Martial Expertise
- Reducing fumble severity and recovering faster from botched attacks.
- Maximizing damage output on confirmed critical hits.
- Recognizing and partially anticipating enemy fighting styles.

## Based on SENSES

### Acrobatics
The body's ability to move with precision through difficult space — vaulting, tumbling, balancing on narrow surfaces, landing without breaking bones. Where Athletics is about power, Acrobatics is about control.

#### Actions that use Acrobatics
- **Tumble** — Move through an occupied space without triggering opportunity attacks.
- **Vault** — Cross obstacles or low barriers without breaking stride.

#### Passive uses of Acrobatics
- Ignoring or reducing difficult terrain penalties.
- Avoiding or minimizing damage from triggered traps.

---

### Stealth
Moving without being seen or heard. Pressing into shadows, timing footsteps to background noise, controlling breathing. The body as something that can simply cease to exist in a room.

#### Actions that use Stealth
- **Sneak** — Move between rooms or positions without alerting nearby creatures.
- **Hide** — Become undetectable in a current location, waiting for an opportunity or letting a threat pass.
- **Ambush** — Initiate combat from concealment for a first-strike advantage.

#### Passive uses of Stealth
- Delaying creature detection when entering a hostile area.
- Maintaining concealment while observing or eavesdropping.

---

### Perception
Noticing things. What is out of place, what is missing, what does not fit. The guard who blinks too slowly. The door that was closed this morning. The smell of something burned, trying to smell like nothing. Perception is the skill that tells you something is wrong before you know what it is.

#### Actions that use Perception
- **Listen** — Detect nearby creatures or events through sound alone.
- **Scout** — Gather information about an adjacent or visible area before entering.
- **Search** — Thoroughly examine an area for hidden objects, traps, or secret passages.

#### Passive uses of Perception
- Detecting hidden objects, traps, and secret passages.
- Avoiding sneak attacks by sensing an attacker's approach.
- Seeing through deception attempts and recognizing lies.

---

### Sleight of Hand
Fine motor control applied with intent: picking locks, disarming traps, palming objects, and manipulating mechanisms in ways their designers did not intend. Where Perception notices, Sleight of Hand acts — quietly and without leaving obvious evidence.

#### Actions that use Sleight of Hand
- **Lockpick** — Open a lock without the original key.
- **Disarm Trap** — Neutralize a triggered or dormant trap without setting it off.
- **Pickpocket** — Lift a small item from a creature without detection.
- **Tinker** — Make small mechanical modifications to devices, weapons, or equipment.

#### Passive uses of Sleight of Hand
- Reducing noise when interacting with mechanical objects.
- Recovering quickly from a failed lockpick or disarm attempt.

---

## Based on MIND

### Arcana
Understanding magic as a system — its rules, its history, its failures, its costs. Identifying spells mid-cast, reading magical writing, understanding what a ritual was designed to do and what went wrong when it did not. Magic is not wonder here. It is a language, and this skill is literacy.

#### Actions that use Arcana
- **Identify** — Determine the nature, school, and approximate power level of a magical effect or enchanted item.
- **Counter** — Recognize and partially suppress or dispel an active magical effect.
- **Scribe** — Transcribe or decode magical writing, runes, or ritual diagrams.

#### Passive uses of Arcana
- Resisting the effects of illusions and mental constructs.
- Recognizing magical traps before triggering them.
- Evaluating the safety of using an unknown magical artifact.

---

### Technology
Understanding machines, systems, and the principles behind them. Repairing, modifying, building, diagnosing — from clockwork mechanisms and combustion engines to things that hum with energies that have no good name yet. In settings that include advanced technology, this skill extends to computers, security networks, automated systems, and anything driven by electricity or code. The world runs on mechanisms. This skill is knowing how.

#### Actions that use Technology
- **Repair** — Restore a broken device or machine to working order.
- **Hack** — Override or bypass digital access controls and security systems.
- **Build** — Construct or modify mechanical devices from available components.
- **Diagnose** — Identify the cause of a malfunction or unusual behavior in a system.

#### Passive uses of Technology
- Recognizing the purpose and threat level of unfamiliar devices.
- Improvising with available parts when purpose-built tools are unavailable.

---

### Investigation
Active, methodical searching for information — examining a crime scene, searching a room for hidden compartments, cross-referencing accounts for inconsistencies. Where Perception notices, Investigation understands.

#### Actions that use Investigation
- **Examine** — Scrutinize a specific object, area, or creature for clues and hidden details.
- **Analyze** — Compare multiple sources of information to identify contradictions or patterns.

#### Passive uses of Investigation
- Retaining and connecting details observed earlier in an encounter.
- In dialogs : some extra lines of dialog may appear.

---

### Alchemy
Understanding the chemical and biological properties of substances, and knowing how to combine, refine, or extract them into new forms. From crude herbal remedies to precise poison compounds to unstable reagents that should not be handled near an open flame.

#### Actions that use Alchemy
- **Brew** — Combine two or more reagents to produce a potion, poison, acid, or other compound.
- **Extract** — Isolate a specific substance from raw natural materials (plants, minerals, creature parts).
- **Analyze Substance** — Identify the composition and properties of an unknown liquid, powder, or vapor.

#### Passive uses of Alchemy
- Recognizing known poisons or reagents on sight or by smell.
- Reducing failure risk and ingredient waste when crafting compounds.
- Partially resisting accidental exposure to toxic substances during handling.

---

## Based on PRESENCE

### Persuasion
Changing minds through reasoned argument — presenting evidence, constructing logic, finding the frame that makes your position feel inevitable. Unlike Deception, Persuasion works best when you are telling the truth. Unlike intimidation, it respects the target's capacity to think. The most durable agreements are made through Persuasion.

#### Actions that use Persuasion
- **Negotiate** — Reach an agreement with an NPC through reasoned exchange.
- **Convince** — Change a creature's course of action or stated position.
- **Rally** — Reframe a failing situation in terms that restore confidence and resolve.

#### Passive uses of Persuasion
- Improving the initial disposition of neutral creatures.
- Softening the consequences of a failed social interaction.

---

### Faith
Absolute conviction in something larger than the self — a god, a principle, a cause, a necessary lie. In a world this dark, faith is not comfort. It is armor. It is also, occasionally, a weapon. The mechanical effect is real regardless of whether the object of faith deserves it.

#### Actions that use Faith
- **Pray** — Appeal to a divine force for a blessing, protection, or small miracle.
- **Invoke** — Establish contact with an outer god, cosmic entity, or powerful spiritual force — at considerable risk.
- **Condemn** — Direct spiritual conviction outward as a curse or sanction against a specific target.

#### Passive uses of Faith
- Anchoring allies against the psychological damage of prolonged supernatural exposure.

---

### Haggle
The art of negotiation, valuation, and trade. Understanding what something is worth, what the other party wants, what they will settle for, and where the deal becomes unprofitable. Not all Presence is noble. Sometimes it is just knowing how to read a merchant's eyes.

#### Actions that use Haggle
- **Bargain** — Negotiate a better price when buying or selling goods.
- **Appraise** — Accurately assess the market value of an item, including fakes and forgeries.
- **Fence** — Sell restricted, stolen, or unusual goods to specialized buyers.

#### Passive uses of Haggle
- Reducing purchase prices in shops.
- Increasing sale prices for goods you carry.
- Detecting overpriced or counterfeit items before completing a transaction.

---

### Aura
The projection of presence, will, and dominance. Every creature can feel a sufficiently fierce aura — in the weight of a voice, the stillness before violence, the silence that falls when someone who knows how to use it enters a room.

#### Actions that use Aura
- **War Cry** — Unleash a shout that instills fear and doubt in enemy ranks while bolstering the confidence of allies.
- **Dominate** — Lock eyes with a single creature and impose a momentary freeze through sheer force of will.
- **Demoralize** — Systematically erode the resolve of an opponent through presence, posture, and controlled aggression.

#### Passive uses of Aura
- Intimidating interlocutors during dialogue without explicit threat.
- Causing hesitation in creatures that would otherwise act immediately.
- Projecting authority that improves compliance from neutral creatures.
