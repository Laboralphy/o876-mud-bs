# Threats

A threat is a dangerous force directed at a creature.
Most threats may be resisted with the adequate skill or ability.
If a threat is resisted, its effects can be halved or even negated.

---

## Saving throws

When a creature is subjected to a threat, it makes a saving throw — rolling 1d20 plus its resisting
skill or ability modifier against a Difficulty Class set by the source of the threat.

### Threat difficulty class

If a creature is the origin of the effect, its abilities may be used as bonus to the difficulty class.
A creature with high abilities is more dangerous, and its attacks are less likely to be resisted

For spells and special abilities, the DC is:

```
DC = 10 + caster's ability modifier + spell level (1–6)
```

The caster's ability depends on the nature of the threat:

| Caster type                                     | Ability used |
|-------------------------------------------------|-------------|
| Arcane caster (wizard)                          | MIND        |
| Divine caster (paladin, cleric)                 | PRESENCE    |
| Instinct-based (dragon breath, natural ability) | SENSES      |

A weak dragon (SENSES +1) casting a level 1 breath produces DC 12. A master wizard (MIND +5) casting a
level 6 spell produces DC 21 — near the upper bound of what trained defenders can reliably resist.

### Shield bonus

Against a **Blast** — a fast-moving physical phenomenon that spreads across an area — a shield provides
real protection. Its armor class value is added to the defender's Acrobatics roll. You can crouch behind
a tower shield against a cone of fire. You cannot crouch behind it against a charm.

No other threat benefits from shield protection. Mental, spiritual, and magical threats bypass physical
barriers entirely.

### Unyielding

Some effects are unyielding. These bypass all saving throws — no skill check is made, no ability check
is rolled. The condition simply applies. Unyielding is reserved for absolute, unavoidable forces: the
stone gaze of a greater basilisk, a curse that admits no defence.

## Threat descriptions

### Blast

*"The fire came in a wave. There was nowhere to run — only the choice between covering up or being swept away."*

A fast-moving physical phenomenon that spreads across an area: dragon breath, a cone of force, a gas
grenade, a burst of ice. It can deal direct damage, knock targets down, root them in place, or do all
three. The key is that it moves faster than a creature can escape — only quick reflexes and good
positioning limit the damage.

**Resisted by:** Acrobatics. **Shield bonus applies.**

---

### Blindness

*"She blinked. The world was still there. She just couldn't see any of it."*

Something extinguishes or overwhelms a creature's sight — magical darkness, a blinding flash, a venom
that seals the eyes. A creature that understands light and perception can partially shield itself: closing
eyes at the right moment, reading the edges of the effect, minimising exposure.

**Resisted by:** Perception.

---

### Charm

*"He was still himself. He just couldn't find a reason to disagree with her."*

A force seizes the creature's will and redirects its allegiances. Magical compulsion, supernatural
presence, a psychic rewrite of who the target considers a friend. Resisted by Aura — the strength of
self that refuses to be overwritten. Armour is irrelevant. A will is not a body.

**Resisted by:** Aura. **Ongoing resistance check triggered when attacked by the charmer.**

---

### Disease

*"The fever broke on the third day. The weakness did not."*

A biological or magical infection that takes hold and progresses. Unlike poison, it worsens over stages
rather than dealing steady damage. Each stage transition is an opportunity to fight it off. Survival —
the practical knowledge of staying alive in hostile conditions — is the relevant skill.

**Resisted by:** Survival.

---

### Magical Device

*"She'd seen enough wands fired in her direction to know that the trick was understanding what they were
about to do."*

A magical or technological device — a wand, a rod, a staff — directed at the target. Unlike raw spells,
devices follow mechanical and magical patterns that can be anticipated. A creature with understanding of
how devices work can recognise the discharge signature and partially counter it.

**Resisted by:** Technology.

---

### Fear

*"Her legs moved before she decided to run. Something older than thought had already made the choice."*

Supernatural terror directed at a creature's instincts rather than its mind. It bypasses reason — the
creature does not decide to be afraid, it simply is, completely and immediately. Faith — conviction in
something larger than the self — is what holds the line when reason cannot.

**Resisted by:** Faith.

---

### Paralysis

*"Every instruction the brain sent arrived and was ignored."*

The body is seized — nerves locked, muscles unresponsive. The creature may be fully conscious and aware
of everything happening. Athletics is the relevant skill: raw physical endurance and the ability to fight
through the mechanism that paralysis exploits.

**Resisted by:** Athletics. **Ongoing resistance check triggered on damage taken while paralysed.**

---

### Petrification

*"Six seconds. That's how long the process took."*

Transformation into stone or an equivalent inert substance. This is not something a creature dodges or
reasons its way out of — it either has the biological fortitude to resist the transformation or it
doesn't. Resisted by a raw Body ability check, not a skill.

**Resisted by:** Body (no skill).

---

### Poison

*"The wound was nothing. What the wound carried was something else entirely."*

A toxic substance entering the bloodstream — venom, alchemical compound, poisonous gas. Alchemy covers
the knowledge of how toxins work, how they move through the body, and what conditions allow them to
take hold. Resistance is checked both at the moment of application and each time the poison would deal
damage.

**Resisted by:** Alchemy. **Checked at application and each poison tick.**

---

### Polymorph

*"When it was over she had four legs, which was two more than she'd started with."*

Forced transformation of physical form. Like petrification, this operates at the level of fundamental
biology — no skill applies, only the raw resilience of the creature's constitution. A creature with
high Body simply resists the transformation through sheer biological stubbornness.

**Resisted by:** Body (no skill).

---

### Spell

*"She felt the magic forming before it fired — a half-second of structure she recognised and disrupted."*

A direct magical attack that doesn't fit a more specific threat category — an arcane bolt, a targeted
curse, a spell that reshapes reality around a single creature. Arcana represents the ability to read
magical patterns, partially counter their structure, and reduce their impact through understanding rather
than brute resistance.

**Resisted by:** Arcana.

---

### Stun

*"The bell rang. The next few seconds were simply absent."*

Momentary overwhelming of the creature's ability to act — a concussive blow, a scrambling magical
pulse, a shock that scatters thought and reflex simultaneously. Discipline is the relevant counter:
the trained capacity to keep functioning despite being hit hard.

**Resisted by:** Discipline. **Ongoing resistance check triggered on damage taken while stunned.**

---

## Drains

Ability drain, attack drain, and armor class drain do not map to a fixed threat. Their resistance is
determined at the point of application by the **nature** of the source:

| Source nature  | Resisting ability |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Supernatural   | PRESENCE          |
| Extraordinary  | BODY              |
| Magical        | MIND              |
| Weapon         | SENSES            |

The same drain can be harder or easier to resist depending on whether it comes from a magical curse,
a venom-coated blade, or an inherent supernatural ability. There is no immunity to drain.
