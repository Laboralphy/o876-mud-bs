# Immunities Reference

Immunities represent an absolute resistance to a particular kind of affliction. Unlike resistances, which merely
blunt the force of an attack, an immunity negates it entirely. No saving throw is required. No partial effect
seeps through. The affliction simply does not take hold.

Immunities can be granted by innate nature — a skeleton feels no poison because it has no blood to poison — or
bestowed through magic, equipment, or circumstance.

---

## Charm

The creature's mind cannot be seized, seduced, or redirected by magical compulsion. Enchantment spells that would
bend a creature's loyalty or cloud its judgment find no purchase here. This does not mean the creature is
unintelligent or free of emotion — only that its will cannot be stolen by magical means.

*Typically possessed by: undead, constructs, creatures without a true mind.*

---

## Disease

The creature's body (or lack thereof) cannot be infected by disease, natural or magical. Parasites find no host,
contagion spreads no further, and the slow rot of plague leaves no mark. This is not merely a strong constitution —
the biology simply does not support infection.

*Typically possessed by: undead, constructs, elementals.*

---

## Fear

The creature cannot be made to flee, cower, or freeze by magical fear effects. Supernatural dread washes over it
without effect. This does not mean the creature is brave — it may be utterly without feeling — only that the
mechanism fear exploits is absent or inert.

*Typically possessed by: undead, mindless constructs, creatures of pure rage.*

---

## Paralysis

The creature cannot be locked in place by paralytic magic or venom. Its body continues to respond to its will
regardless of what a paralyzing effect commands. This often reflects a physiology so different from flesh — or so
reinforced by magic — that the usual nerve-seizing mechanisms cannot function.

*Typically possessed by: undead, constructs, oozes.*

---

## Petrification

The creature cannot be turned to stone or otherwise transmuted into an inert solid state by magical effects. Its
form resists the transformation entirely, whether through magical protection, an alien physiology, or some property
of its substance that defies lithification.

*Typically possessed by: creatures already composed of stone or metal, certain undead, entities outside normal
biology.*

---

## Poison

No venom, toxin, or poisonous gas affects this creature. Its blood does not carry poison to vital organs, or it
has no vital organs to poison, or its constitution is so extreme that even magical toxins are neutralised on
contact. A creature immune to poison can walk through a cloud of lethal vapour without coughing.

*Typically possessed by: undead, constructs, elementals, certain magical beasts.*

---

## Root

The creature cannot be magically anchored in place against its will. Spells and effects that pin a creature to
the ground or freeze it in its tracks — without fully paralyzing it — fail to grip. The creature continues to
move freely.

*Typically possessed by: incorporeal creatures, entities with no fixed physical form.*

---

## Stun

The creature cannot be dazed, staggered, or rendered unable to act by stunning effects. Blows that would leave
an ordinary creature reeling, spells that scramble motor function, concussive force that shuts down higher
thought — none of these produce the stunned condition in this creature.

*Typically possessed by: constructs, certain undead, creatures with distributed or alien nervous systems.*

---

## Blindness

The creature cannot be magically blinded. Whether its sight is supernatural, redundant, or replaced entirely by
other senses, effects that extinguish normal vision leave it unimpaired. It may not even use eyes at all.

*Typically possessed by: creatures that navigate by tremorsense, blindsight, or purely magical perception.*

---

## Critical Hit

The creature has no vital points that a skilled attacker can exploit. There are no organs to puncture, no arteries
to sever, no joints to shatter for extra effect. A roll of 20 on the attack die is still a solid hit — but it
deals no more damage than any other blow, and the automatic-hit rule does not apply.

*Typically possessed by: oozes, elementals, constructs, undead without discrete anatomy.*

---

## Granting Immunities

An immunity can come from several sources:

- **Innate property** — part of the creature's fundamental nature, set at creation and never removed.
- **Equipment property** — a magic item worn or wielded that confers the immunity while equipped.
- **Active effect** — a spell or ability currently running on the creature. The immunity lasts only as long as the effect does.

When multiple sources grant the same immunity, only one is needed. Losing one source does not remove the immunity
if another source remains active.
